# Engineering Portfolio

Welcome! I'm **Vishal Talwar**, a software engineer with over 18 years of experience designing enterprise applications, distributed systems, platform infrastructure, and workflow-oriented software.

This repository is more than a collection of projects or a traditional résumé. It documents my journey as an engineer—how my thinking evolved through different roles, the lessons I learned, and the engineering principles that continue to shape the systems I build today.

Over the years, I have intentionally sought opportunities that expanded my understanding of software engineering. My career began with enterprise web applications, progressed through platform development and long-term product ownership, and eventually led to distributed systems, workflow orchestration, metadata-driven architectures, and AI infrastructure.

Today, I am most interested in building reliable, maintainable systems that enable both applications and engineering teams to evolve with confidence.

# Engineering Principles

Throughout my career, a few principles have remained consistent:

- Learn continuously.
- Build systems, not just features.
- Prefer simplicity over cleverness.
- Design for long-term maintainability.
- Treat observability as part of the architecture.
- Generalize recurring problems into reusable frameworks.
- Optimize for reliability before optimization.
- Share knowledge generously.

# Why This Portfolio Exists

Traditional résumés summarize where someone has worked.

They rarely explain **how an engineer thinks**.

This portfolio attempts to bridge that gap.

Each document focuses not only on what I built, but why it was built that way, the trade-offs involved, and the lessons that shaped my engineering philosophy.

My hope is that readers come away with a deeper understanding of how I approach software design, architecture, collaboration, and continuous learning.
